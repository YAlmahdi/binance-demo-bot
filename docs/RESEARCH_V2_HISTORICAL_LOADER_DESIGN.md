## 1. Availability and entry timing

Use exact integer UTC milliseconds. Let `S = 300,000 ms`, `T` be the scheduled UTC hour, and `D = T − 1 ms` be the decision row timestamp. The decision candle opens at `T − S`, reports `close_time = D`, and completes at `T`. The causal cutoff is inclusive at `T`. A candle's open and reported close are event timestamps. Original availability is when the exact completed version was demonstrably accessible to the decision process, supported by contemporaneous receipt/version evidence and a defensible clock and processing boundary. `retrieved_at` records when a later copy was actually acquired. It has a separate evidence identity and does not establish historical availability.

| Contract | Causal treatment and evidence | Status and permitted claim |
|---|---|---|
| `REQUIRE_VERIFIED_ASOF` | Existing synthetic `assess_source` checks a source/revision-bound `ORIGINAL_ASOF_RECEIPT` assertion, `T <= original_receipt <= retrieved_at`, and an internally supported `VERIFIED` state. Strict mode requires `original_receipt = T` for the decision candle. `feature_at` independently requires each completed prefix row's `available_at <= T` and each `ObservedCandle` requires `available_at >= close_time + 1`. | Implemented as synthetic checks. Future strict historical use requires authentic per-row version evidence or an authenticated batch commitment covering the exact causal prefix with equivalent per-row bounds. |
| `EXPLORATORY_CLOSED_BAR_APPROXIMATION` | Proposed conditional analysis using the pinned snapshot's completed candle values through `D` and the same origin, grid, quality and warm-up rules. `close_time + 1` is a modeled completion boundary only. Preserve actual retrieval, original receipt if known, per-row revision and `UNKNOWN` where evidence is missing. Emit a separate approximation basis and next-open assumption. | Existing enum and synthetic metadata vocabulary only. Operational use inactive and unapproved. Any result is historical exploration with final-snapshot and latency assumptions. |

In the current metadata contract missing strict evidence is `INSUFFICIENT / ORIGINAL_RECEIPT_UNKNOWN`, an internally supported receipt later than `T` is `INSUFFICIENT / RECEIPT_LATE`, and an asserted `VERIFIED` without supporting evidence is `INVALID / ORIGINAL_AVAILABILITY_UNSUPPORTED`. A pre-completion receipt or receipt after retrieval is `INVALID / INVALID_SCHEMA_OR_TIME`. Malformed or misbound evidence is `INVALID / INVALID_EVIDENCE`; identity and revision mismatches retain their existing diagnostics. `INVALID` dominates `INSUFFICIENT`. A valid claim of `UNKNOWN` can pass synthetic approximation metadata validation. An evidenced late `VERIFIED` receipt is still insufficient in that metadata mode. Synthetic consistency supplies neither independent authority nor an operational adapter.

For a future strict reader, prove each exact row version was received after its own completion and usable by `T`. A covering batch can attest to bounds for its committed row set. A later revision requires its own original evidence. Missing or clock-ambiguous proof remains `INSUFFICIENT / ORIGINAL_RECEIPT_UNKNOWN`. Proven lateness stays `RECEIPT_LATE`. Conflicting source, row version, clock chronology or fabricated evidence is `INVALID`. For the proposed approximation, missing timely proof may be accepted only as an explicit counterfactual assumption. Positive evidence of lateness, or of a material version change after `T` with no authenticated timely version available, excludes that affected decision with its late or revision reason. Unresolved revision identity blocks the affected input. Retain all raw versions. Select no version based on outcome information and never populate `ObservedCandle.available_at` with a modeled timestamp.

`T` is also the frozen earliest modeled next-bar entry. A receipt at `T` meets the inclusive feature cutoff but provides no lead time for processing, submission or execution at the recorded next open. Track execution feasibility separately as unverified without independent evidence of the full timing and fill path. A receipt after `T` cannot support entry at that open. The recorded opening price is outcome-side data and is never an admission input. Do not shift `T`, assume an executable price, or change latency, fees or slippage. A measured delay policy or alternative entry convention requires its own review.

Recommend explicit strict mode for engineering and any future prospective track, with no fallback. Consider approximation only through a separately approved, preregistered historical-exploration policy. Keep evidence, decision inventories, reporting and evaluation roles separate across modes. Authentic timely historical receipts can establish availability of those exact versions at the recorded times. Decisions reconstructed without those receipts or from later revisions lack that proof. A modeled next-open price supplies no evidence of fill feasibility, even when original receipts are verified. Prospective evidence further requires the applicable pre-event freeze, original recording and sealed inspection controls, so retrospective reconstruction cannot qualify.

## 2. Independent full-horizon coverage

For one symbol and one scheduled `D`, define `E = T + 48 × S = T + 4h`. The certificate covers exactly `[T,E)`, with ordered opens `T + i × S` and closes `T + (i + 1) × S − 1 ms` for `i = 0…47`. The decision bar is a separate bound anchor and does not count toward 48. The last close is `E − 1 ms`. An observation at open `E` belongs to the following interval. A clipped horizon, early exit, equal row count with a duplicate, or declared `horizon_end` cannot satisfy the grid. Extra rows in an authorized larger input may be selected out by the fixed interval predicate. An extra row in the certificate's own purported exact slice is invalid.

Before read, require `[T,E)` and its anchor and prefix dependencies to be fully authorized. For partition eligibility apply the existing ex-ante bounds independently. Training retains `E <= evaluation_start − 4h` after overlap purging. Evaluation retains `E <= evaluation_end`. Any known end-of-window cutoff is a calendar rule common to comparators. Do not base it on observed row counts or slide the window. The independent coverage verdict uses an explicit audit observation cutoff `K`, separate from the evaluation window and from the causal cutoff `T`. The event grid cannot mature before `E`. Exact completed versions must also be evidenced as available to the audit by `K`. Audit maturity is `max(E, latest proven availability to the audit of the 48 bound versions)`. A verified timestamped acquisition of an immutable exact slice can establish an upper bound for audit availability, with its basis disclosed. It cannot prove historical original receipt or backdate maturity. At `K = E`, certification is possible only if the exact 48 versions are evidenced by that instant. Later audit evidence may establish retrospective coverage at its own cutoff, even for an evaluation window ending at `E`.

| Proposed coverage result | Deterministic criterion |
|---|---|
| `SUFFICIENT` | Authorized, exactly bound, structurally valid grid and authenticated audit availability by `K`. Report the maturity timestamp or proven upper bound and its basis. |
| `INSUFFICIENT / HORIZON_NOT_MATURE` | `K < E`, or an otherwise valid required version is evidenced to become available only after `K`. |
| `INSUFFICIENT / HORIZON_OBSERVATION_UNPROVEN` | Required audit availability or row provenance is missing, even if candidate rows exist. |
| `INSUFFICIENT / CANDLE_GAP` | A mature, correctly scoped, well-formed slice is missing any expected open. Report missing grid positions. |
| `INVALID` | Malformed timestamps, incomplete or inconsistent candle shape, duplicate or unordered rows, out-of-slice rows, wrong identity, mismatched revision/commitment or conflicting evidence. Use `INVALID_SCHEMA_OR_TIME`, `SOURCE_CONFLICT`, `UNAUTHORIZED_DATES`, or the applicable identity/evidence reason. An as-yet-uncompleted bar at the audit cutoff supplies no usable observation. |

Authorization failure stops before row inspection. For authorized inputs report all independently established reasons in fixed order, with `INVALID` taking precedence. Untrusted precomputed `SUFFICIENT` flags are inadmissible. Validate raw ordered rows without sorting, deduplicating or interpolating. Do not emit a consumable certificate on a refusal. Diagnostics can retain exact missing positions and row evidence references within the authorized output scope. Proposed new reason codes belong to the new diagnostic, leaving existing enums intact.

Bind a passing certificate to authorization and query identities, immutable snapshot, candle source and revision, symbol, interval, `D/T/E/K`, projection and schema, time basis, decision anchor identity and exact ordered 48 row/version identities. Bind `row_id = symbol:D` alongside that context. A canonical versioned commitment must cover exact row values, identities, bounds, order and cutoff evidence with unambiguous types and serialization. Revalidate the binding where a result is consumed. The feature artifact separately commits to its exact causal prefix; do not key it by the outcome-slice digest or make its status depend on that digest. In a new snapshot that changes future rows, causal value and admission remain equal when the authorized as-of prefix and calendar are identical. Provenance still records each actual snapshot. Never transplant a certificate across snapshots, rows or revisions based on equal counts or a matching bare `row_id`.

Keep four channels distinct:

1. **Causal feature readiness** uses only `[P,T)`, frozen feature rules and the chosen availability evidence. Receipt and correction metadata can establish which exact versions were usable by `T`. No future market rows, audit cutoff, outcome completeness or funding state enter the causal calculation or admission. Preflight and read scopes must be separable so the horizon can be unauthorized, unqueried, incomplete or immature while the causal channel remains valid.
2. **Opportunity inventory and eligibility** schedule both symbols at each authorized hour and retain abstention reasons. Apply only known calendar restrictions and causal gates at admission. Preserve scheduled counts and the causal coverage denominator, including rows that later lack usable outcomes.
3. **Future label validity** would require a mature certificate, partition eligibility, independently adequate funding and any frozen execution/input rules. A missing bar, late outcome version or missing funding excludes exact paired labels with reasons. It never yields NO TRADE, removes an earlier causal opportunity, or shortens a label. The certificate computes no label or payoff.
4. **Financial funding readiness** is a separate full-interval evidence claim. Candle completion establishes no settlement obligation, mark, timing or economics. Unknown funding can coexist with valid candle coverage and causal readiness.

## 3. ATR prefix and gap policy

Pin a 5-minute UTC origin `P` before examining outcomes for the intended scope and immutable source lineage. Use the first expected grid open of the explicitly approved study context as the common origin for paired BTC/ETH research, with independent per-symbol rows. The first observed row cannot choose the origin. Register the origin rationale and prefix-basis evidence, including the exact first source row/version and authorized context boundary. A deliberately bounded origin defines the estimator for that study, without asserting it is market inception or that prior history was available. The actual `P` awaits snapshot and scope evidence. Any prospective origin must respect its registered accumulation and receipt contract.

At `D`, require every row with open in `[P,T)`, exactly `(T − P) / S` ordered rows from `P` through `T − S`. Require at least 200 completed and admissible bars including the decision bar. The first true range is `high − low` at `P`. Each subsequent true range is the maximum of `high − low`, `abs(high − prior_close)` and `abs(low − prior_close)`. The ATR14 seed at zero-based index 13 is the existing arithmetic mean of the first 14 true ranges. Subsequent ATR is `(13 × prior_ATR + current_TR) / 14`. Carry forward the entire contiguous prefix from the same origin for each decision, using the frozen numeric implementation. A fold, chunk or query boundary does not start a new seed. A rolling last-200 slice or predecessor outside the authorized origin changes the estimator. Keep the minimal first engineering batch free of checkpointing.

A valid 199-row prefix is WARMUP. An absent origin row, interior gap or missing decision row makes that decision and later decisions crossing the defect unusable under the pinned origin. Earlier decisions with valid prefixes retain their causal result. A duplicate, disorder, invalid schema/time or mixed revision fails closed. If separately authorized authoritative evidence fills a defect in a new immutable revision, rerun from the same `P`; strict admission still requires timely version evidence for each affected historical decision. Otherwise stop dependent decisions. A new origin after a gap would require a separately approved study revision, with a new origin, warm-up and explicit comparability break. This specification permits no automatic resumption, truncation, repair or reseeding.

Preserve raw rows and their arrival/order and revision evidence. Hard-invalid inputs include malformed types, invalid grid, unfinished candles, nonfinite OHLCV, nonpositive prices, inconsistent OHLC, negative volumes/trades and source conflicts. Valid zero volume, flat bars and `(high − low) / close > 0.05` are documentary flags under current quality rules. Preserve them in a structurally valid prefix; a zero-volume or flat decision candle still causes frozen causal abstention, as does a nonpositive prior-20 volume mean or unusable numeric result. Do not alter thresholds, smooth spikes, delete flagged bars or promote a documentary flag to a historical realism filter. Source realism and any future use of flags beyond existing rules require separate evidence and review.
